# Vestra brand

**Positioning:** evidence-led company research for tokenized private markets on Solana.
**Voice:** precise, calm, candid about uncertainty.
**Status:** active

## Visual direction

Use a dark, comfortable-density research workstation. Borrow broad interaction patterns from crypto trading apps (clear asset search, price hierarchy, concise market data, dense but scannable panels) while keeping Vestra’s identity, composition, icons, and wording original. Do not reproduce any app’s exact layout, logos, illustrations, or proprietary assets.

## Palette

- **Canvas:** deep graphite, `#0C0F0E`
- **Raised surface:** `#121715`
- **Panel surface:** `#171D1A`
- **Borders:** `#27302C`
- **Primary text:** `#F0F4F1`
- **Secondary text:** `#A0AAA4`
- **Muted text:** `#707B74`
- **Vestra mint:** `#9AF0C0` (primary accent, active states, links)
- **Positive:** `#70D6A3`
- **Negative:** `#F08A8A`
- **Warning:** `#E9BF72`

Green/red are semantic only. Price movement must also include signed text or symbols; color is not the only cue.

## Typography and numbers

- Use a neutral sans serif for product UI and clear medium-weight headings.
- Use tabular numerals for prices, percentages, and counts.
- Use monospaced styling sparingly for addresses and technical identifiers.
- Keep a compact but comfortable hierarchy; never reduce body copy below 12px.

## Shape and motion

- Subtle borders define panels; avoid strong shadows and glass effects.
- Use consistent medium corner radii and deliberate spacing.
- Use brief, functional transitions; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Data labels should be calm and direct. Avoid speculative hype and buy/sell calls.
