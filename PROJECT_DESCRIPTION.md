# Vestra

Vestra brings PreStocks company news, token data, and market replay into one research desk. Choose a company from the PreStocks catalogue to see its description, issuer link, token mint, token price, reference mark, valuations, and supply alongside relevant headlines.

Bull, Bear, and Neutral examine the same evidence from different perspectives. A Council checks their arguments against the sources, summarizes where they agree or disagree, and highlights what is still unknown. You can follow the citations or play an audio version of the Council’s summary.

Market replay follows the selected PreStocks mint to a Solana USDC pool. It displays the pool’s candles in LuxAlgo Vela, lets you inspect prices around dated headlines, and includes a simple 5/20-day moving-average backtest with adjustable cost assumptions.

The backtest uses discovered pool data. Pool prices are separate from PreStocks’ quote, so its results do not represent PreStocks returns. Headline replay shows timing, not proof that a headline caused a price move. Vestra is a read-only research tool, not a trading recommendation. PreStocks tokens provide economic exposure under PreStocks’ terms, not ownership rights in the referenced companies.
